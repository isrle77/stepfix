Privacy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

The short version

This site has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no ads. The free builder keeps your work in your own browser. The widget you put on your website doesn't track your visitors.

The free builder

Everything you type in the builder stays on your device. It's saved in your browser's local storage so you can come back to it, and it is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

The widget on your website

The widget runs entirely in your visitors' browsers. It sets no cookies, collects no personal data and makes no requests to us. It only loads the tree and any images that you yourself put in it. The free version shows a plain link to isrstechportal.com; nothing is sent unless a visitor clicks it.

Hosting

This website is hosted on Render. Like any web host, Render may process standard technical data such as IP addresses in server logs to deliver and protect the site. We don't use those logs to identify or track you.

Purchases

The full version is sold through Gumroad. When you buy, Gumroad processes your payment and email address under its own privacy policy. We receive the order details Gumroad shares with sellers so we can deliver the product and answer support questions.
